## Result for the live project

No screened result supplies the missing constructive four-dimensional Yang--Mills chain: a nontrivial continuum limit with reflection-positive OS reconstruction, a constructive-real Hilbert/GNS space for the target state, a cutoff-uniform survival estimate for the original compact-group action, and calibration of an actual physical transfer mass. The one Yang--Mills-tagged entry is about classical boundary conditions in asymptotically AdS space; it is not a Euclidean measure construction or a continuum gap theorem.

### Five closest overlaps

1. Yan Ru Pei, **Uniform displacement bounds and Gibbs limits for periodic one-dimensional Riesz gases**, [arXiv:2609.28503](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28503). The abstract states a particle-displacement variance bound of order `beta^-1`, uniform in particle number, exponential displacement tails from log-concavity, and simple stationary periodic thermodynamic limits satisfying Gibbs equations. The method compares inverse random Hessians with deterministic electrical flows on a transient long-range network. Possible input: a model for turning a finite-volume estimate into stationary Gibbs-limit statements, and a useful example of precisely stated uniformity. Limitation: one-dimensional neutral Riesz particles with a scalar convexity/Hessian structure; no nonabelian compact-group variables, reflection positivity, OS reconstruction, YM measure, or physical transfer mass. Its numerical or asymptotic conclusions cannot certify the continuum theorem.

2. Lucus Brady and Ryan Grady, **Functoriality of the KSGNS Construction for Intertwiners of Strict Positive C*-Correspondences**, [arXiv:2605.08004v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.08004). The v3 full text (dated 23 September 2026) treats KSGNS as an idempotent endofunctor on a category of strict positive C*-correspondences and proves unitary unique equivariant dilation. The introduction explicitly identifies ordinary GNS as the special case with correspondence to `C`; the technical setting is Hilbert C*-modules, completely positive maps, intertwiners, and equivariant correspondences. Possible input: a clean categorical reference for functoriality/naturality questions if the project later packages its state representation in C*-algebraic terms. Limitation: it assumes the C*-algebraic structures and strict positivity needed for KSGNS; it does not construct the project’s constructive-real OS state or show that its finite-volume representations converge to a continuum representation.

3. Shiyu Cao, Zhian Jia, and Sheng Tan, **Topological quantum color code model on infinite lattice**, [arXiv:2601.12409v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.12409). The full text constructs the quasi-local spin C*-algebra, a pure unique translation-invariant ground state, and finite-temperature KMS states for a commuting-projector color-code model; it studies DHR sectors. Possible input: an example of the operator-algebraic separation between local dynamics, ground states, and thermodynamic states. Limitation: a two-dimensional discrete spin model with commuting stabilizers. A text search of the full paper found no spectral-gap result; neither its model nor its state construction gives the nonabelian four-dimensional continuum YM limit.

4. Donghoon Kim and Tomotaka Kuwahara, **Entanglement area law in interacting bosons from the Bose-Hubbard model to phi^4 theory and beyond**, [arXiv:2411.02157](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.02157). The paper proves a one-dimensional area law for bosonic systems with unbounded on-site energy and power-law interactions decaying faster than `r^-2`, with an MPS approximation guarantee. Its proof reduces to a bounded quasi-local effective Hamiltonian and applies an approximate-ground-state-projector method. Possible input: techniques for controlling an unbounded local Hilbert space and recognizing exactly which decay and gap hypotheses an area-law theorem consumes. Limitation: one spatial dimension, an assumed spectral gap and Hamiltonian ground state, and a scalar/bosonic lattice model. It establishes neither a four-dimensional continuum field nor a Yang--Mills mass gap.

5. Robert M. Wald and Xinkang Wang, **Alternative Boundary Conditions in Asymptotically Anti-de Sitter Spacetimes**, [arXiv:2609.10805v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10805). The full text studies local, AdS-invariant conservative boundary conditions and their phase spaces; it extends the electromagnetic conditions to classical Yang--Mills in AdS. Possible input: boundary-condition and symplectic-flux bookkeeping for a distinct classical YM problem. Limitation: Lorentzian asymptotically AdS boundary data and classical phase space, rather than Euclidean R^4, the constructive measure, or a positive OS Hamiltonian gap.

## Use and limits

- Best possible later references from this listing: the KSGNS paper for categorical naturality of Hilbert-module dilation; the boson area-law paper for explicit control of unbounded local energy under its one-dimensional gapped hypotheses; the AdS paper only for classical boundary-condition bookkeeping.
- The Riesz paper's volume-uniform bounds are a methodology analogue only. They depend on a scalar one-dimensional interaction, log-concavity, and a network comparison, not on the project's original compact-group Haar integral.
- The reviewed papers provide no producer for the project's original-Haar square certificates, no cutoff/volume-uniform signed covariance response, and no theorem identifying a stationary finite-cutoff state with a continuum OS theory.
- No numerical calculation was run or used as a proof claim. This research task changed only this report and ran no proof checks; it changed no Lean statement, hypothesis, axiom gate or roadmap.
