## 2. Kinetic-influence MC (V2 probe)
Slice = periodic 3³ spatial links, both neighbouring slices fixed (Haar-random or aligned V ≡ 1). The quantity is
the response of E[U_ℓ] (ℝ⁴ chordal vector) to rotating one kinetic boundary link V_{ℓ₀} by e^{θX}, computed as
Cov(U_ℓ, ∂_θ log f), with bias-corrected magnitudes (20 batches). |ΔE[U]| ≤ W₁, so these are lower bounds.

| w | boundary | sweeps | direct (ℓ = ℓ₀) | largest off-diagonal | noise sd per link | reading |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.35 | Haar | 6000 | 0.0417 (≈ 0.97u) | 0.0018 | 0.0014 | no visible spread |
| 1/2 | Haar | 6000 | 0.1176 (≈ 0.94u) | 0.0053 | 0.0041 | no visible spread (≤ 5% per link, at noise) |

Sums over all 80 other links are dominated by residual noise and are not quoted as signal.

**Readings.**
- Steps 2–3: typical boundaries show no in-slice amplification above noise. The per-neighbour mean response is
  consistent with the expected O(u³) gauge-disorder suppression. So **TB-DOB is not refuted at steps 2–3**,
  and its budget (A ≲ 2) is not obviously out of reach. The open part is the **worst case** over boundaries.
- Step 4: the direct term alone uses 92% of the budget (Haar boundary), and neighbour responses are visible.
  **TB-DOB at the 1/12 target is numerically implausible at step 4** (not a proof: 0.384 < 0.417).
- The reduction this gives (new, CANDIDATE): **step 2 ⇐ SLICE-AMP(1/2)**, where SLICE-AMP(w) says that the 3D slice
  conditional measure (spatial plaquettes at nome w, kinetic fields of strength w from both sides) has worst-case
  W₁ response amplification A ≤ 2.3·(u/κ_kin). This is a 3D statement replacing a 4D one. It is still beyond
  single-site Dobrushin (in-slice 12κ ≈ 1.5 > 1), so it needs a block or finite-box certificate in 3D.
- U(1) test: TB-DOB / SLICE-AMP are group-agnostic (U(1)-true at matched activity). Steps 2–3 are not
  non-abelian evidence in any case (the matched U(1) is confined there).
