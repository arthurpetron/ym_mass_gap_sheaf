These sources concern a planar \(\mathcal N=4\) super-Yang--Mills

mass gap, and therefore cannot discharge any Clay-target ledger item.

## Reusable certificate pattern

The report describes a finite constrained candidate space, a bootstrap
calculation, a related form-factor route, and an independent validation of the
result.  The accompanying finite data release is useful as a provenance model:
publish the exact finite data and ancillary evaluator material, then retain a
reproducible manifest/checksum for the release.

The corresponding constructive protocol for this repository is:

1. Keep the signed-channel and direct-response routes separate until they are
   connected by a typed equality on the SAME completed carrier and observable.
2. Generate exact finite rows (Rust is permitted only as a certificate
   generator), and require a Lean evaluator to check every stated constraint.
3. Use a second recoding or independent route and held-out cutoff/placement
   rows as validation; these checks do not become continuum evidence.
4. Ledger all parameter dependence, reconstruction moduli, and untested
   assumptions.  A finite manifest is not a physical-rate band, an OS/GNS
   bridge, or a continuum theorem.

## Consequence for the active frontier

The changing-nome SU(2) family already supplies one completed same-object
channel and a scale-dependent strict form margin.  The next useful certificate
is therefore a *same-family* scale law comparing that explicit margin with the
vanishing spacing.  A second derivation may validate the finite algebra, but it
must not substitute a different carrier, transfer, or observable.
