## 1. The construction at a glance

We construct the continuum SU(2) Wilson measure `μ_∞` as the
Daniell–Kolmogorov projective limit of the family of finite-volume
lattice measures `{μ_k}` generated by the UV-direction block-spin
flow.  The construction has four pieces:

1. **Projective system** (`formal/continuum-measure.tex` §2).  The
   inverse system of configuration spaces
   `... ← Ω_2 ← Ω_1 ← Ω_0` with arrows the block-spin coarsenings
   `T_b`, and the inverse system of measures `... ← μ_2 ← μ_1 ← μ_0`
   with arrows the pushforwards `(T_b)_*`.

2. **Consistency** (`formal/continuum-measure.tex` §3).  The Wilson
   family satisfies `(T_b)_* μ_{k+1} = μ_k`, by construction of the
   RG transformation: the renormalized action at scale `k` is the
   effective action obtained from scale `k+1` by integrating out the
   intra-block fluctuations.

3. **Tightness** (`formal/continuum-measure.tex` §4).  Each
   `Ω_k = SU(2)^{E_k}` is compact (product of compact factors).
   Tightness is therefore trivial — the compactness of SU(2) is the
   structural payoff that makes the projective limit work without
   additional analytical input.

4. **The extension theorem** (`formal/continuum-measure.tex` §5).
   The Daniell–Kolmogorov theorem on compact-Hausdorff inverse
   systems gives a unique Borel probability measure `μ_∞` on
   `Ω_∞ = lim ← Ω_k` whose pushforward to each finite stage is the
   corresponding `μ_k`.

## 2. The mass-gap bridge: σ̂_∞ > 0 lifts to μ_∞

The Phase-7.1 framework theorem
`SigmaHatLimit.sigma_hat_uv_limit_positive` proves the Q'-rational
form

    ∃ ε > 0, ∀ N, σ̂_N ≥ ε.

Taking the limit `N → ∞` (a bounded monotone limit in the rational
domain), `σ̂_∞ ≥ ε > 0`.  Composed with the area-law bound at every
finite stage and the projective-limit consistency
(Corollary 4.2 of the paper), this gives:

> **Proposition** (`formal/continuum-measure.tex` Proposition 6.1).
> Every Wilson loop `C` resolved at scale 0 has
> `⟨W_R(C)⟩_{μ_∞} ≤ C_R · exp(-σ̂_∞ · A(C))` with `σ̂_∞ ≥ ε > 0`.

This is the load-bearing input for Phase 8.1 (OS axioms — exponential
clustering) and Phase 9 (mass-gap theorem).

## 3. The Lean-paper hybrid decision

Milestone 7.2 explicitly opens the question of whether to build
`lean/YMGap/Constructive/Reals.lean` (Bishop-style constructive Cauchy
reals + measure theory) or accept a paper-only existence proof at this
boundary.

**Decision: paper-only existence at 7.2; Reals.lean deferred to 7.3.**

Rationale (also recorded in `formal/continuum-measure.tex` §7):

1. The Daniell–Kolmogorov extension uses the Riesz representation
   theorem on `C(Ω_∞)`, which constructively requires Bishop measure
   theory of compact Hausdorff spaces — a major build-out far beyond
   the current Q'-rational stack.

2. The mass-gap statement of Phase 9 can be expressed against the type
   signature `ContinuumMeasure` of
   `lean/YMGap/Continuum/ContinuumMeasure.lean`; the paper proof of
   existence lets Phase 9 land in Lean without waiting on
   Constructive/Reals.

3. Milestone 7.3 of the work-plan reserves the option of building
   `Constructive/Reals.lean` later (with the σ̂_∞ : Real promotion of
   `sigma_hat_uv_limit_positive` as one motivating use case); the
   current decision does not foreclose that.

## 4. What's in the Lean module

`lean/YMGap/Continuum/ContinuumMeasure.lean` exposes:

  * `ProjectiveSystem (M : Nat → Type)` — the inverse-system data,
    abstract over the constructive-measure carrier `M`.
  * `is_consistent sys pick` — the Kolmogorov consistency predicate.
  * `ContinuumMeasure M` — the data of a projective system + a
    consistent family + an abstract `LimitMeasure` + the per-stage
    projection morphisms.
  * `su2_continuum_measure ...` — a smart constructor for the SU(2)
    instance.
  * `continuum_measure_signature_axiom_clean` — sentinel `True`
    theorem reporting empty axiom list (documents that the module
    introduces no analytical sorries).

The data is **abstract**: the `M k` carrier, the `LimitMeasure`
carrier, and all maps are inputs to `su2_continuum_measure`.  The
paper-level analytical content (Theorems 3.2 and 4.1) is the
justification for inhabiting the structure with the SU(2) Wilson
data; supplying those proofs constructively is the 7.3 build-out.
