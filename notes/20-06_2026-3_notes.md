2. Important kill-check: the crude C_4^2 numerator is not enough
The proved numerator estimate is valuable, but do not try to close the quotient by simply combining
|N_s| \lesssim u^2(s+1)C_4(s)^2
with
g_0\gtrsim u^{-3/2}.
On active modes,
C_4(s)\sim \frac{y_s}{u}, \qquad s+1\sim u^{-1/2}.
So the crude numerator behaves like
u^2(s+1)C_4(s)^2 \sim u^2\cdot u^{-1/2}\cdot u^{-2}y_s^2 = u^{-1/2}y_s^2.
Meanwhile
D_s=g_0\hat c_s \gtrsim u^{-3/2}\cdot u^{-1/2}e^{-O(y_s)} = u^{-2}e^{-O(y_s)}.
Dividing gives only
\frac{|N_s|}{D_s} \lesssim u^{3/2}e^{O(y_s)}y_s^2.
But the target is
u^2P(y_s).
So the crude numerator misses by a factor of roughly u^{1/2}, and it lacks the right Gaussian-in-y_s shape.
That is not bad news. It tells us exactly what the next proof must capture: the extra half-power and the Gaussian shape come from the local-CLT quotient structure, not from the coarse C_4^2 numerator.
So the existing numerator theorem is a sanity check. The quotient proof needs a sharper, denominator-aware estimate.

3. Best proof chip: quotient local CLT for SU(2)
For p=2, use the Weyl integral or the coefficient-equivalent diagonal decomposition.
The analytic quotient is
\frac{g_s/g_0}{\hat c_s/\hat c_0}.
The desired statement is
\boxed{ \frac{g_s/g_0}{\hat c_s/\hat c_0} = 1+u^2E(y_s), \qquad |E(y_s)|\le P(y_s). }
That is exactly
|r^{\mathrm{rel}}_s| \le u^2P(y_s).
The cleanest mathematical route is:
g_s = \int_{SU(2)} K_\tau(U)^2\chi_s(U)\,dU, \qquad g_0 = \int_{SU(2)} K_\tau(U)^2\,dU.
Then
\frac{g_s}{g_0} = \mathbb E_{\nu_{\tau,2}}[\chi_s(U)],
where \nu_{\tau,2} is the probability measure with density proportional to K_\tau(U)^2.
Near the identity write
U=\exp X,\qquad |X|=O(\sqrt\tau).
For SU(2)\cong S^3,
\chi_s(\exp X) = (s+1) \left[ 1-\frac{C_4(s)}{6}|X|^2 + O\!\left(C_4(s)^2|X|^4\right) \right].
The measure K_\tau^2\,dU is Gaussian to leading order with variance proportional to \tau/2, plus curvature/Jacobian corrections.
The s=1-retuning should cancel the full first-order term. What remains is
u^2P(uC_4(s)).
That is the discrete local-CLT chip.
