# BF2.1: general reflection-positive boundary carrier

Date: 2026-07-24

Status: **closed by the enlarged reflected-Gram route**.  This is not a

## 1. Result

BF1.5 falsified the cone generated by closed full-slab surface scalars.
BF1.6 repaired one selected sector by cutting each surface at the
time-reflection plane and retaining its exposed boundary tensor.  BF2.1
identifies the general carrier selected by that repair:

\[
 {\cal C}_{\partial\Lambda}
 =
 \{A=A^\ast:\ A\succeq0\}
\]

on the complete, fusion- and intertwiner-resolved boundary Hilbert space.
At infinite representation cutoff the finite matrices are replaced by
positive trace-class kernels in the corresponding weighted direct sum.

There are two separate statements.

1. The exact plaquette-coupled heat-kernel Gibbs law gives a
   positive-semidefinite reflected Gram kernel on every finite family of
   positive-half observables.
2. Exact Haar integration and coarse boundary restriction act on this cone
   by a completely positive map

   \[
   A\longmapsto \Phi(A)=\sum_r K_r A K_r^\ast .
   \]

Both statements retain magnetic indices, intertwiner multiplicities,
orientations, and signed \(F/R\) changes of basis.  A signed recoupling
coordinate may have negative entries; it acts by unitary congruence and
cannot change the cone.

This supplies BF2's required general reflected-Gram/SOS theorem and a
fusion-closed positive carrier.  BF3 must still construct the actual
corrected-slab Kraus operators, their joint coarse-intertwiner transport,
and the literal finite-cutoff remainder.  Complete positivity says what
order those objects must preserve; it does not construct or estimate them.

\[
 64\text{ lower patches}

If \(x\) and \(y\) are two lower fillings with the same cut signature

\[
 x\#_\gamma y

 x\cup\gamma\cup\theta(y).

\[
 \coprod_{\gamma}
    \{x_0^\gamma,x_1^\gamma\}^2

Thus the 128 gluings are distinct and are exactly the 128 closed binary

## 3. General heat-kernel reflected-Gram theorem

### Theorem

Let \(\Lambda\) be a finite lattice gauge complex with a reflection
\(\theta\) across a cut, and let \(G=SU(2)\).  Assign positive heat times
\(t_p>0\), symmetrically under reflection, and let

\[
 d\mu_\Lambda(U)
 =
 Z^{-1}\prod_{p\subset\Lambda}K_{t_p}(U_p)
 \prod_{e\subset\Lambda}dU_e
\]

be the exact heat-kernel lattice gauge law.  For any finite family
\((F_i)\) of square-integrable, gauge-invariant positive-half observables,
retaining all open magnetic and intertwiner indices at the cut, the matrix

\[
 G_{ij}
 =
 \int\overline{\theta F_i(U)}F_j(U)\,d\mu_\Lambda(U)
\]

is positive semidefinite.

The statement is unchanged by an orthonormal fusion-tree change, including
its signed \(F/R\) matrices.  Every finite boundary representation cutoff
is a PSD principal compression of the full kernel.

### Proof

The proof has three exact factorization steps.

First, plaquettes contained strictly in the two open halves occur in
reflection pairs.  The heat kernel is a strictly positive continuous
function for \(t>0\), so the positive-half factor

\[
 W_+(U_+)=\prod_{p\subset\Lambda_+}K_{t_p}(U_p)^{1/2}
\]

may be absorbed into every \(F_i\).  Reflection supplies the conjugate
negative-half factor.

Second, cut plaquettes have a lower and an upper open holonomy, say
\(A_p\) and \(B_p\).  Peter--Weyl gives the positive-kernel expansion

\[
 K_t(A B^{-1})
 =
 \sum_{\lambda\in\widehat{SU(2)}}
 d_\lambda e^{-tC_2(\lambda)}
 \sum_{m,n}
 D^\lambda_{mn}(A)
 \overline{D^\lambda_{mn}(B)}.
\]

Every coefficient \(d_\lambda e^{-tC_2(\lambda)}\) is strictly positive.
The product over cut plaquettes is therefore a sum over a complete
multi-index \(\alpha\) of a positive-half feature
\(\phi_\alpha(U_+)\) times its reflected conjugate.  The index \(\alpha\)
contains the representation labels and both magnetic indices.  Resolving
gauge invariance at cut vertices replaces magnetic products by complete
orthonormal bases of
\(\operatorname{Inv}(\bigotimes_\ell V_{\lambda_\ell}^{\epsilon_\ell})\);
it does not discard an index.

Third, integrate the positive and negative internal links.  With

\[
 \Psi_{i,\alpha}(u_\partial)
 =
 \int_{\Lambda_+^\circ}
 F_i(U_+)W_+(U_+)\phi_\alpha(U_+)\,dU_+^\circ,
\]

Fubini and Haar-reflection invariance give

\[
 G_{ij}
 =
 \frac1Z
 \sum_\alpha
 \int
 \overline{\Psi_{i,\alpha}(u_\partial)}
 \Psi_{j,\alpha}(u_\partial)
 \,du_\partial .
\]

Hence, for every vector \(c\),

\[
 \sum_{i,j}\overline{c_i}G_{ij}c_j
 =
 \frac1Z
 \sum_\alpha\int
 \left|\sum_i c_i\Psi_{i,\alpha}\right|^2du_\partial
 \ge0.
\]

This is the required Gram/SOS representation.  A signed orthonormal
recoupling matrix \(U\) replaces \(G\) by \(UGU^\ast\), which preserves
positive semidefiniteness.  A boundary cutoff projection \(P\) replaces it
by \(PGP\), also PSD.  The full heat series converges absolutely for
\(t>0\), so these finite compressions converge to the stated kernel. \(\Box\)

## 4. Blocking theorem and the correct finite-cutoff rule

Let \({\cal H}_f\) and \({\cal H}_c\) be the complete fine and coarse
boundary Hilbert spaces, with all fusion and intertwiner indices.  Exact
gluing followed by Haar integration has a Stinespring form

\[
 \Phi(A)
 =
 \operatorname{Tr}_{\rm int}(VAV^\ast)
 =
 \sum_rK_rAK_r^\ast.
\]

Consequently \(\Phi\) maps the full PSD cone into the full PSD cone.  This
is the precise sense in which the BF2 carrier is stable under exact
spacing-changing blocking.

This theorem also fixes a subtle but essential cutoff rule.  If \(P\) is a
finite coarse carrier projection and \(Q=1-P\), then

\[
 \Phi(A)
 =
 P\Phi(A)P
 +P\Phi(A)Q
 +Q\Phi(A)P
 +Q\Phi(A)Q.
\]

The retained block and the outside diagonal block are PSD compressions, but
the two off-diagonal blocks carry genuine coherence.  They may not be
discarded or replaced by a scalar remainder.  A finite carrier is exactly
closed only if

\[
 QK_rP=0\qquad\text{for every Kraus index }r.
\]

BF1.6 verified this condition in its selected cutoff-two disk by finding a
literal zero remainder.  BF3 must compute the whole corrected-slab
operators and decide it in the actual physical sector.  At arbitrary
cutoff, use a compression of the exact physical heat-kernel law; do not
replace the law itself by a character truncation whose pointwise
positivity has not been proved.

## 6. What this does not resolve

1. **Entrywise (DP) remains open.**  PSD is a statement about all quadratic
   forms, not about every matrix entry or every fixed recoupling channel.
   Negative entries are compatible with a PSD kernel.
2. **No Birkhoff contraction follows from complete positivity.**  BF4 still
   needs strict positivity and a finite projective diameter over fixed
   physical distance.
3. **The physical block kernel is not yet constructed.**  BF3 must return
   the whole corrected-slab Kraus family, coarse-intertwiner map, mass, and
   coherent remainder.
4. **There is no cutoff- or volume-uniform remainder estimate.**
5. **RG-I7, RG-I8, RG-I9, continuum construction, OS reconstruction, the
   Hamiltonian gap, and the all-groups quantifier remain open.**

The progress is nonetheless structural.  The positive cone needed by the
Birkhoff--Hopf program is now named without decategorifying away the
coherent data: it is the PSD cone of the physical reflected boundary
kernel.  The next question is no longer whether signed channel coordinates
are entrywise positive.  It is whether the exact corrected-slab CP map has
a cutoff- and volume-uniform projective diameter after grouping over fixed
physical distance.
