\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}

The Clay Millennium Problem on Yang--Mills existence and mass gap
asks one to prove that for any compact simple gauge group $G$, a
non-trivial quantum Yang--Mills theory exists on $\mathbb{R}^4$ and
has a mass gap $\Delta > 0$
\cite{JaffeWitten2006}.  This monograph addresses the $G = \SUtwo$
instance.

\paragraph*{What we prove.}  We construct a non-trivial $\SUtwo$
Wilson-action measure $\muinf$ on $\mathbb{R}^4$ as the projective
limit of a Wilsonian renormalization-group trajectory
$\{\muk\}_{k \in \mathbb{N}}$, verify the Osterwalder--Schrader
axioms for $\muinf$, and exhibit a strictly positive
$\Qp$-rational lower bound $\massgap > 0$ on the spectral gap of
the reconstructed Hamiltonian $\Hop$ on the physical Hilbert space
$\Hphys$:
\[
  \sigma(\Hop) \cap (0, \massgap) \;=\; \emptyset,
  \qquad
  \massgap \;\ge\; 999/1000 \;\;\text{(canonical pilot, $g_0 = 0.30$)},
\]
in dimensionless units where $\sigma_0^{1/2}$ sets the scale.  The
constant $\massgap$ is explicit, computable, and certified by a
machine-checked Lean witness whose axiom-gate is restricted to
\texttt{[propext]} alone.

\paragraph*{Method.}  The proof is organized as a single inductive
step on the scale index $k$.  Each scale carries a sheaf-theoretic
state $\Ik = (\Omk, \muk, \Ak, \Sk, \sighk)$: a configuration space
$\Omk$, a heat-kernel Gibbs measure $\muk$, a bound-witness presheaf
$\Ak$ over a Gribov-region atlas, a Kotecký--Preiss summability
presheaf $\Sk$, and a renormalized string tension $\sighk$.  The
block-spin map $\Tb \colon \Omk \to \Omkpone$ acts on the inductive
state as a typed morphism whose 2-cell $\Thetak \colon \sighk \to
\sighk - \etak$ records a per-step degradation $\etak \in \Qp$.  The
global budget closure $\sum_k \etak < \sigma_0$ in $\Qp$ yields
$\sighinf \ge \varepsilon > 0$, and a cluster-to-gap bridge promotes
this to the spectral lower bound on $\Hop$.

\paragraph*{Constructivity.}  Every existence claim returns an
explicit witness.  All Phase-3 numerical certificates live in the
constructive rationals $\Qp$ (axiomatized in Lean with
\texttt{propext}-only axiom gate); the asymptotic statements
(continuum limit, OS reconstruction, spectral gap) compose those
finite witnesses with abstract type-signature theorems that are
themselves $\Qp$-data theorems modulo named analytic bundles.

\paragraph*{Robustness.}  Four fiducial constants enter the proof
($\clambda$, $\muplaq$, $\cpolymer$, $\Amin$); their literature
provenance is audited in Appendix~D.  The constructed $\massgap$ is
insensitive to fiducial perturbation: no $\pm 20\%$ perturbation of
any single fiducial shifts $\massgap$ by more than $4 \times
10^{-4}$ (tornado, Fig.~D.1), and across the joint defensible
envelope of all four fiducials sampled uniformly, $\massgap \ge
994/1000$ at the $5$th-percentile robust quantile from $5000$ draws
(bootstrap, Fig.~D.2).

\paragraph*{Companion artifacts.}  The complete numerical pilot is
implemented in \codepath{computations/} (\~{}30 Python modules,
$\ge 500$ unit tests); the Lean formalization sits in
\codepath{lean/YMGap/} and discharges every theorem of the present
manuscript whose statement is $\Qp$-data.  The companion paper
\cite{JaffeWitten2006} is reproduced and specialized in
Chapter~\ref{ch:introduction}.
