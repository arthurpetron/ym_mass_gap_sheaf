\noindent\emph{One page for the reviewer in a hurry: the theorem, the
number, the witnesses, the proof in three lines.}

\begin{infobox}[main][breakable=false,title=Main theorem (SU(2) mass gap)]
Let $\Hop \ge 0$ be the self-adjoint Hamiltonian obtained by
Osterwalder--Schrader reconstruction of the continuum $\SUtwo$
Wilson measure $\muinf$ on $\mathbb{R}^4$
(\Cref{ch:continuum-measure}--\ref{ch:os-reconstruction}).  Then
there is an explicit, computable, $\Qp$-rational $\massgap > 0$ with
\begin{equation*}
  \sigma(\Hop) \cap (0, \massgap) \;=\; \emptyset,
  \qquad
  \massgap \;\ge\; \frac{999}{1000}
  \quad\text{(canonical pilot, $g_0 = 0.30$)},
\end{equation*}
in dimensionless units where $\sigma_0^{1/2}$ sets the scale
(\Cref{ch:mass-gap-theorem}, \cref{thm:mass-gap-su2}).
\end{infobox}

\paragraph*{The explicit number.}  The bound is a strict $\Qp$-rational
lower bound, not a sharp value.  At two resolutions and two
heat-kernel conventions:
\begin{center}
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.2}
\begin{tabular}{lccc}
\toprule
Pilot & $\sighinf \ge$ & $\massgap \ge$ ($/1000$) & $\massgap \ge$ ($/10000$) \\
\midrule
canonical ($g_0 = 0.30$) & $9991/10000$ & $999/1000$ & $9996/10000$ \\
Wilson ($g_0 = 0.50$)    & $9997/10000$ & $999/1000$ & $9994/10000$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
The physical gap is expected to be a factor $\approx 3.74$ larger (the
SU(2) glueball-to-string-tension ratio); the value of $\massgap$ is not
the point --- its being \emph{positive, explicit, and machine-checked}
is (\Cref{ch:mass-gap-theorem}, \S\ref{sec:sharpness}).

\paragraph*{The proof in three lines.}  The proof composes three
constructions along the scale index $k$ (\Cref{fig:proof-composition}):
\begin{enumerate}\setlength{\itemsep}{1pt}
  \item \textbf{Typed inductive step} (\Cref{ch:sheaf-framework}--%
    \ref{ch:pilot-su2}): each scale carries a sheaf-theoretic state
    $\Ik$; the block-spin morphism $\Tb$ preserves it up to a
    $\Qp$-rational per-step degradation $\etak$, verified by the
    five-criterion pilot at finite truncation $K = 3$.
  \item \textbf{Continuum limit} (\Cref{ch:uv-trajectory}--%
    \ref{ch:continuum-measure}): the budget $\sum_k \etak < \sigma_0$
    closes with an explicit uniform-geometric tail
    (\cref{lem:uniform-decay}), giving $\sighinf \ge \varepsilon > 0$;
    the lattice measures admit a projective limit $\muinf$.
  \item \textbf{OS reconstruction + cluster-to-gap}
    (\Cref{ch:os-axioms}--\ref{ch:os-reconstruction}): $\muinf$
    satisfies the Osterwalder--Schrader axioms; the area-law--to--gap
    bridge $m \ge \sqrt{\sighinf}$ promotes string-tension positivity
    to the spectral gap of $\Hop$.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph*{What is machine-checked vs.\ paper-level.}  The
$\Qp$-rational spine --- the per-step budget, the closure
$\sum_k \etak < \sigma_0$, and the arithmetic
$\massgap^2 \le \sighinf$ --- is fully formalized in Lean.  The
analytic bundle it composes against --- the Kotecký--Preiss
certificates, the Daniell--Kolmogorov projective limit, the OS-axiom
verifications, and the operator-theoretic reconstruction (Stone's
theorem) --- is supplied at the paper level, against Lean
type-signature interfaces.  This split is made visual in
\Cref{fig:proof-composition} and itemized in the open-items register
of Appendix~\ref{ch:appx-roadmap}.
