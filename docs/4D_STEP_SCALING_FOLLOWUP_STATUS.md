## Stage A — U(1) control (finished 2026-10-17 ~16:00 PDT)
Data: compact U(1) Wilson action, same flow/clover-E pipeline as SU(2); r(L,beta,c) = t^2 <E> beta / N_lat_u1(c,L), with N_lat_u1 the exact free-field lattice mode sum (L=4..16; beta = 1.2, 1.4, 1.7, 2.2, 3, 6, 12 at all L, L=16 added in this stage; high-beta beta = 100, 400, 1600 at L = 4, 6, 8). Full tables: `simulations/gf_step_scaling/results/followup/A_u1_r.md`, `ff_u1_rust_vs_free.md`.
* **Free-field limit (beta -> infinity):** MC r = 1 within 0.1–1.7 sigma (0.3–1% errors) in all 27 (L, beta, c) cells at beta = 100, 400, 1600 (e.g. L=8, beta=1600, c=0.3: 1.0013(43); L=4, beta=400: 0.9987(45)). The deterministic mode sum is also matched by an independent exact Gaussian sampler (`free_field_u1.py`), and the Rust Metropolis/flow at L=4, beta=3 by an independent NumPy checkerboard-Metropolis + RK4 implementation (`u1_numpy_check.py`; the larger NumPy batch was not rerun because of the <= 3-process rule — only the L=4 comparison was done). So normalisation, flow and clover E are right: **no pipeline/normalisation bug found.**
* **Finite beta:** r - 1 = k/beta with k = (r-1)*beta ~ 0.3–0.4 at beta = 3, 6, 12 and *essentially L-independent* (beta=3, c=0.3: 0.43, 0.41, 0.36, 0.32, 0.33 for L = 4, 6, 8, 12, 16; beta=12: 0.33, 0.38, 0.37, 0.25(5), 0.29(9)). This is the O(1/beta) non-Gaussian (compact-action / nonlinear flow) correction of the lattice theory, a coupling-renormalisation-type offset that cancels in the step ratio; it is not a finite-volume or 1/L^2 effect and not a bug.
* **Step ratio Sigma/u = r(2L)/r(L)** (c=0.3; the "deficit" of the earlier U(1) control): beta=3: 0.9796(43), 0.9757(64), 0.9930(87) for 4->8, 6->12, 8->16; beta=6: 0.9928(32), 0.9941(50), 0.9934(72); beta=12: 1.0030(31), 0.9892(55), 0.9937(73). The 8->16 ratios are consistent with 1 at all beta >= 2.2 (within 1.5 sigma; c=0.4 likewise: 1.0056(140), 0.9971(116), 0.9927(114)); the 2–4% 4->8 deficits at beta <= 3 shrink with L (0.9796 -> 0.9930 at beta=3) and vanish at beta >= 6 within ~2 sigma. At beta = 1.2–1.7 (near the U(1) transition, beta_c ~ 1.01) the ratios are 0.88–0.97 and non-monotone: non-perturbative / strongly coupled, not Coulomb-phase control data.
* **Verdict (A):** the earlier 2–12% U(1) deficit is a **coarse-lattice cutoff (and near-transition) effect that shrinks with L**, not a pipeline/normalisation bug. Residual: 8->16 ratios at moderate beta are 0.7–1.3% below 1 with ~0.7–0.9% errors, i.e. not distinguishable from 0; U(1) therefore bounds the pipeline systematic at the 8->16 level at ~1% (MC, not a bound in the rigorous sense). Consequence for SU(2): the 8->16 and 6->12 shifts are not contaminated by an O(5–10%) normalisation artefact; the a/L = 1/4 pair remains the outlier (as in Stage B).
