## What the swap actually decomposes into

The Penrose route proves `|C(G)| ≤ τ(G)` (signed connected-spanning sum ≤ spanning-tree
count) by deletion–contraction, modulo the one uninhabited recursor `DCHyp`. The BK route
the user proposed is two steps:

1. **the forest-interpolation IDENTITY** `φ^T(X) = Σ_F ∫_{[0,1]^edges} ds W(F,s) ∏(pair)`, `W ≥ 0`;
2. **the tree-graph bound FROM positivity**: since `W ≥ 0` and activities are pointwise ≥ 0
   for repulsive weights, `|φ^T| ≤ Σ_{trees} ∏|pair|`.

I implemented and separated these to see where the real cost is.

### The positivity leg (step 2) — PROVED, and genuinely trivial

`treeMajorant_le_treeCount`: for `|w| ≤ 1`,
`treeMajorant w n edges = Σ_{T tree} ∏_{ℓ∈T}|w_ℓ| ≤ ofNat(treeCount n edges)`.
One list induction (`listSum_le_len` + `edgeAbsProd_le_one`); no case analysis. This is the
*entire* "pointwise positivity/monotonicity" content of step 2. **It compiles in ~5 lines of
real work.** Confirms the user's instinct that positivity is cheap — but this was never the wall.

### The identity (step 1) — the named residual `ForestInterpolation`, UNINHABITED

`ForestInterpolation w` carries exactly the BK tree-graph inequality
`|C(G)| ≤ treeMajorant w`. Left uninhabited. Its honest cost is either:
- the BK interpolation **integral** over `s ∈ [0,1]^{edges}` (a constructive multivariate
  integral + fundamental theorem of calculus) — **absent from the `Q'`/`CReal` core**; or
- the Penrose partition-scheme / deletion–contraction combinatorics (`DCHyp`).

These are the **same difficulty class**. The forest identity is NOT independently cheaper here.
In a Mathlib setting BK genuinely beats Penrose (the tree structure falls out of a Taylor
expansion, avoiding the hard bijection); the advantage evaporates in a core with no integration.

## The machine-checked refutation of the naive premise

`triangle_refutes_positivity` (by `decide`) + `triangle_values`: on the triangle `K₃` with
the hard-core weight `ζ = -1`,

| quantity | value | meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `spanCount` = Σ_{A spanning} ∏\|ζ\| | **4** | positivity/triangle-inequality bound applied to `C` directly |
| `treeCount` = τ | **3** | the target bound |
| `\|signedCount\|` = \|C\| | **2** | the true signed value |

`4 > 3`: applying positivity **to `C` itself** overshoots τ — it is useless for `|C| ≤ τ`.
`2 ≤ 3`: the bound holds only through the sign cancellation (`4 → 2`). **Conclusion: the
cancellation is essential and cannot be "replaced by positivity."** It must live inside the
forest IDENTITY (encoded by the tree structure of the interpolation), which is precisely why
step 1 remains the wall. BK's positivity is positivity of the *forest weights `W(F,s)` in the
identity* — not a positivity that removes the cancellation.
